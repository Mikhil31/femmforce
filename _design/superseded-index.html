About us

Barriers get removed by structures, not by sentiment.

FemmForce empowers women with essential skills, leadership opportunities, and support to excel in diverse fields. By addressing barriers and promoting inclusion, we aim to create a society where women thrive and achieve remarkable success.

Our programmes foster talent development, boost confidence, and facilitate recognition — across business, employment, the home, and communities that have had access to none of it.

Training

Nine programmes, three hundred trainers.

FemmForce delivers training to corporates and institutions through a network of more than 300 qualified trainers. Industry experts contribute a portion of their earnings back into our programmes — which is how the training arm funds the work nobody pays for.

POSH Training

External members for Internal Committees under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013. Delivered in multiple languages.

Soft Skill Training

Communication, teamwork, conflict resolution, time and stress management, adaptability and workplace culture.

IT & Technical Training

Programming, software development, system administration, networking and cyber security, with certification tracks.

Career Coaching & Counselling

Five-dimensional career assessment, one-to-one counselling in Bangalore, and a concrete execution plan.

Sales Training

A gender-inclusive approach to goal-setting, first impressions, presentation structure and results-oriented messaging.

Leadership Training

Conflict resolution, leading innovation, virtual leadership, delegation, coaching for performance and building trust.

Customised Training

Programmes built to a client's industry and roles — entrepreneurship, import and export, compliance, crisis management.

Campus to Corporate

Communication, presentation, goal setting, business etiquette and interview readiness for students entering the workforce.

Train the Trainers

Training need analysis, public speaking, content and objective writing, delegation and teamwork for practising trainers.

How we fund the work

Two channels, deliberately.

Grant Wave

Grants and donations from CSR heads, corporates, NGOs, consortiums and foundations who want to contribute directly to the programmes. This is what pays for the women who can't pay.

Skill Fund

Paid training delivered to corporates and institutions. Industry experts contribute a share of their earnings, which means the commercial arm sustains the charitable one rather than competing with it.

Businesses seek efficiency and implementation, rather than just talk about feminism. Uma G Reddy, Trustee and founder

FemmForce was founded on that observation. The vision is to be the stepping stone that helps women rise to new heights, break barriers, and compete in industry with real passion — a system designed to break the conditioning and restrictions women face, and to meet the standards industry actually expects.
