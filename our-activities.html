Ten things we actually do.

FemmForce is always in action. These are the activities that carry the work, across all four groups of women the trust supports.

Our activities

From mentoring to empanelment.

Each activity runs across every cohort. Follow a row through to the page where that work actually happens.

01Mentorship ProgramsOne-to-one mentoring across every cohort — founders, graduates, mid-career professionals and women returning after a break. 02Networking EventsIntroductions that would not otherwise happen: peers, employers, buyers, and the institutions that hold the budgets. 03Training / Skill Building WorkshopsNine structured programmes for organisations, plus workshops run directly for the women in our beneficiary groups. 04Access to FundingRoutes to grants, donors and CSR budgets for women building a business, and funded places for women who cannot pay. 05Leadership DevelopmentDecision-making, team building, emotional intelligence and the confidence to lead with integrity. 06Recognition and AwardsVisibility for women who are breaking barriers and challenging norms in their fields — the mavericks and the trailblazers. 07Collaboration and PartnershipsNine categories of partner, from corporates and government to knowledge partners and volunteers, each contributing something different. 08Counseling and SupportCareer counselling, five-dimensional assessment, mental-health resources and legal guidance depending on what is needed. 09Business Support / Vendor EmpanelmentSupplier diversity and vendor empanelment, so that women-led businesses reach the procurement lists they are usually missing from. 10Community BuildingA network that keeps working after the programme ends — alumni, peer groups and the people who hire from them.
